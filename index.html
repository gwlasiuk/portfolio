Employee Onboarding

Featured at DevLearn Demofest 2024, this project spans the entire employee onboarding process from receiving a welcome postcard and invitation to learning, ILT and video-integrated orientation, and finally an interactive activity and course-based, self-paced onboarding program. This program seeks to give employees a cohesive message on organizational culture and purpose, while also being uplifting and fun.

  • 1,000+ new hires welcomed
  • DevLearn Demofest 2024

Cashier Training

This comprehensive learning program trained 5,000+ cashiers across multiple stores on essential practices and duties such as handling money, bagging, customer and employee safety, self check-out maintenance, and different transaction types. I created an interactive, virtual cashier terminal that simulated the ones being used in stores so employees could practice ringing up a variety of transactions.

  • 5,000+ cashiers trained
  • Interactive terminal simulation

Small Growers Outreach

Partnering with University of California Davis, this program aimed to teach thousands of small farmers statewide about current food safety and security regulations through accessible online courses and train-the-trainer slide decks. It not only taught food-safe practices, such as water sampling, but also explored emerging technologies and trends in agriculture.

  • Thousands of farmers reached
  • UC Davis partnership
