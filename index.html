View Projects

Manager Basecamp

Winner of the Docebo Inspire award for best UX/UI design. This project involved creating a new manager development program for hundreds of new and existing managers within the organization, including the design of the program's landing pages, self-paced courses, PDF flipbook reference guide, and video interviews of current leaders.

Employee Onboarding

Featured at DevLearn Demofest 2024, this project spans the entire employee onboarding process from receiving a welcome postcard and invitation to learning, ILT and video-integrated orientation, and finally an interactive activity and course-based, self-paced onboarding program. This program seeks to give employees a cohesive message on organizational culture and purpose, while also being uplifting and fun.

Cashier Training

This comprehensive learning program trained 5,000+ cashiers across multiple stores essential practices and duties such as handling money, bagging, customer and employee safety, self check-out maintenance, and different transaction types. I created an interactive, virtual cashier terminal that simulated the ones being used in stores so employees could practice ringing up a variety of transactions.

Small Growers Outreach

Partnering with University of California Davis, this program aimed to teach thousands of small farmers state-wide current food safety and security regulations through accessibly online courses and train-the-trainer slide decks. It not only taught food safe practices, such as water sampling, but also explored emerging technologies and trends in agriculture.
